# Generated outputs

Running `python demo.py` creates the following reports under `outputs/`:

- `coldguardian_incidents.csv` — incident classifications, illustrative score, component values, and action text.
- `coldguardian_rescue_plan.csv` — candidate facility or verification action.
- `coldguardian_experimental_forecast.csv` — linear extrapolation estimates when sufficient readings exist.

These outputs are regenerated from bundled synthetic input data. They are not production reports, live telemetry, validated predictions, or authority to move or dispose of inventory.
